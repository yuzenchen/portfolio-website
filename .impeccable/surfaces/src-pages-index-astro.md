# Home page

Scope: the single-page home route (`src/pages/index.astro`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: owners and senior managers across industries, mostly non-technical, deciding whether Yuzen can turn their repetitive daily work into systems that run themselves. Success is trust; the free 15-minute consultation is the primary action, mail the secondary.

Proof: Wama' House real numbers only (reconciliation 120 to 20 minutes, 0 manual interventions, one-click 7-11 waybill) and the owner-confirmed before/after descriptions in PRODUCT.md. The 80% estimate is gone from the site.

Constraints: the dark terminal palette in `src/styles/tokens.css`, the YZ logo, no emoji anywhere, phone FAQ animations play only on tap, static site with Formspree, no new libraries. Deferred items listed in PRODUCT.md must not appear, not even as placeholders.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Say the outcome in an owner's words, then prove it with one live system. The page leads with the promise "員工每天重複做的事，交給系統自動完成" and backs it with the Wama' House reconciliation race and three measured figures, refusing engineer vocabulary and unmeasured claims.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground, slate panels, one mint accent that only ever means automated, done, or the primary action. Pill buttons with the hard mint shadow, mono for measured numbers and step labels, outlined section numerals, 18px panel corners, bars and rails as the recurring figure (race lanes, process track).

STORY: The visitor reads the promise, sees the race panel and the result row prove it with real numbers, reads how each automation replaced manual work in the case bento, scans three services in plain language, sees the four-step way of working, clears two doubts in the FAQ, and books the free 15 minutes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav with the brand block (name over "流程自動化 × 系統開發") and five Chinese links ending in the mint 聯絡我 pill. Left column: two-line headline, second line mint, description, primary "免費諮詢 15 分鐘" opening the booking dialog, secondary text link "看實際案例 →". Right column: compact race panel with static final clocks 2 小時 / 20 分鐘 and five-segment bars that play the 6:1 time-lapse. The three-card result row starts directly below.

FORM: Owner-copy refresh of the race layout, per the owner's brief and design canvas of 2026-11-12. Signature interaction: the race bars at a true 6:1 ratio plus the single 120 to 20 countdown in the result row. seed: none (unavailable)
